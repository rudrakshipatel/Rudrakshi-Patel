Cortex

The game that makes you a better doctor

A clinical-reasoning game where you’re the only doctor on duty. You take a history, order tests, reach a diagnosis and choose the treatment, and you’re scored on how well you reasoned rather than on what you memorised. Dr. Vix, a fox mentor, guides you through each case.

  • Case flow of patient, tests, results, diagnosis, treatment and outcome across 12+ departments. Ordering too many tests costs you
  • Up to 100 XP per case, daily streaks, leagues, an energy system and time-limited emergency cases
  • Native SwiftUI app with a companion admin app. The backend runs on Supabase edge functions, scheduled jobs and push notifications
  • Marketing site and launch waitlist live at cortexcases.com

Auree

The shared event camera

Auree turns an event into a shared roll of film. Every guest shoots from their own angle, and nothing is visible until the host’s reveal moment. Then the whole roll develops at once, for everyone.

  • The host sets the roll: guest limit, shots per person and when it reveals (live, when the event ends, or a delayed drop)
  • Guests never download anything. They join by QR code or link, through an App Clip on iPhone or the browser on Android
  • Free for up to 5 guests, then a one-time purchase per event, with no subscription
  • Landing site, invite links and an SEO wedding-ideas journal at auree.live

03Web · Game2026

DocRiddles

A daily medical mystery game

There’s one clinical case each day. You reveal clues one at a time and try to name the diagnosis in as few as you can, a bit like Wordle for medicine. When the game ends you get an explanation of the case and an emoji grid to share.

  • Wrong guesses reveal the next clue automatically. Streaks, win rate and clue distribution are saved locally, so no account is needed
  • Hidden admin panel for scheduling cases, with bulk JSON upload that queues one case per day
  • Supabase with row-level security. The public API never sends the answer to the browser

Contractions

A labor contraction timer for iPhone

A calm app for a stressful moment. You tap once when a contraction starts and again when it ends. The app records durations and intervals and shows the pattern on a timeline.

  • Timeline of each session, plus a 5-1-1 guideline prompt with clear medical disclaimers
  • Hospital bag checklist and a “loved ones” quick-contact list
  • Data stays on the device. Subscriptions and restore go through the App Store

Chirpy

A puzzle alarm clock for iPhone

An alarm that won’t stop until you solve a puzzle, and one puzzle makes you get out of bed and scan a real object. There’s no account, analytics or tracking, and all your data stays on the phone.

  • Five wake-up puzzles: Math, Typing, Color Match, Find the Color and Scan an Object
  • The scan puzzle uses Apple’s Vision framework on the device. Each frame is checked and then thrown away
  • Privacy policy and support site written and designed alongside the app

About

Approach & toolkit

I like projects where the details matter: a clue revealed at the right moment, an alarm that actually gets you up, a privacy policy that’s short enough to read.

I work across the whole product. That means designing the interface, building the app, setting up the database and writing the support pages that go with it. My projects so far include native SwiftUI apps, cross-platform Flutter and full-stack web built on Next.js and Supabase.

Wherever I can, I build offline-first, keep data on the device and use on-device ML instead of sending data to a server. Software should earn trust and not ask for it.
